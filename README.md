## SAVELO

Savelo is a budget-first, AI-powered travel planning platform that builds your perfect trip from what you have by adapting to every traveler’s needs with personalized itineraries, verified accessibility to support inclusivity, curated cultural discovery, and a gamified reward system that turns every conscious travel choice into meaningful impact.

## SAVELO FRONT-END

Savelo app made with Flutter framework on the front-end side.
